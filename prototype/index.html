helix

Every morning Helix gives each person one clear answer: how hard to train and how much to eat. It reads recovery and energy from Apple Watch, weighs meals with the iPhone's LiDAR camera, and uses only free, on-device tools.

Preview another morning

The Today screen takes the colour of your readiness, so you know the answer before you read anything.

Who's using Helix

The Watch shows the same colour and score, live heart rate while you train, and the protein left for today. A set logged on the Watch appears on the iPhone straight away.

How it works

Apple Watch
HealthKit supplies active and resting energy, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep stages, VO₂ max and body mass. Calorie targets come from each person's measured burn.
LiDAR portions
ARKit scene depth maps the height of food above the plate. Volume times food density gives grams.
Food recognition
Apple Vision and an open-source Core ML model run on the phone. Nutrition values come from USDA FoodData Central, bundled offline.
Barcodes
The VisionKit scanner looks products up in Open Food Facts, a free and open database.
Coach
Apple's on-device Foundation Models on iOS 26, with a rule-based coach on older phones.
Family and friends
Every person has their own targets, goal and diet. Data stays on their own phone.

This prototype uses generated sample data and saves profiles in your browser only.
