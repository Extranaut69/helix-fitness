Prototype · sample data

Helix
Fitness

Each person gets their own plan. Helix reads Apple Watch recovery and energy data, measures meals with the LiDAR camera, and adjusts calories, macros and training every day. It runs on open-source and on-device tools only, so nothing needs a paid service.

Who's using Helix

The Watch app shows live heart-rate zones while you train, your readiness score, and protein left for the day. Logging a set on the Watch updates the iPhone straight away.

Apple Watch
HealthKit provides active and resting energy, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep stages, VO₂ max, workouts and body mass. Each person's calorie target comes from their own measured burn, not a generic formula.
LiDAR portions
ARKit scene depth builds a height map of the plate. Volume × food density gives grams, so portions are weighed instead of guessed.
Food recognition
Apple Vision + an open-source Core ML model (Food-101 class, swappable) runs on the phone. Nutrition comes from USDA FoodData Central (public domain) bundled offline.
Barcodes
VisionKit scanner + Open Food Facts, the free, open product database with 3M+ items.
AI coach
Apple's on-device Foundation Models (iOS 26, free, private), with a rule-based coach as fallback on older phones.
Family & friends
Every profile has its own targets, goals and diet. Data stays on each person's phone and can sync through their own iCloud.
